HThis manual
  1. Crack terminals. There are 12. Each gives your squad one letter of the passphrase, scrambled.
  2. Reach the Nexus Core in the centre with at least 8 letters and enter the phrase. A wrong guess locks your squad out of the Core for 45 s.
  3. Hold the ring. The right phrase starts an upload: 90 s with all 12 letters, +15 s for every letter you're missing (150 s with 8). Cracking more terminals during the upload speeds it up.
  4. It only runs while more of your squad stands inside the 14 m ring than any single rival squad. Tied or outnumbered: it pauses. Nobody inside: it pauses. Progress is never lost.
  5. NEXUS fights back: a purge on the Core, drone waves every 6 s, more at 25 / 50 / 75 %, and the Hush at 50 %.
  6. The first finished upload wins and ends the game. Everyone else is ranked by points. If time runs out, the highest score wins.

Walk up to a terminal and press E. The screen rarely holds the answer. The city does.

  • Read every wall, listen to every echo, talk to whoever is still awake.
  • Keep your eyes open when the city glitches.
  • Some terminals need more than one of you. A squad of one gets a solo version (and more time).
  • Everything you find is kept under I.

Cameras

Wall cameras sweep a cone. If one sees you, drones within 60 m come running. Crouched and still, they can't see you. E on a camera switches it off for 60 s.
